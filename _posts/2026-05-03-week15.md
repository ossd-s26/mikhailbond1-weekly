# Our Presentation
Wow! I can’t believe the entire semester is finished - it feels like we just started looking into P5.js. Our presentation went very well; it was a great way to show the class what we accomplished.

When all 4 of us were up there talking, I realized how knowledgeable we had become on P5.js. We were able to talk about it for 20 minutes, and then respond to any questions that came up.

As a group, we really grew as both developers and friends. I am very proud of what we contributed to the P5 and learned about general open source development. 

# Other presentations
I found it interesting to watch the differences in difficulty each group had in contributing to their projects. Some groups, like KeyCloak and NanoBot, had projects that had very active maintainers. They made multiple PRs and submitted issue reports without too many issues. Other groups, such as Oppia, really struggled to claim issues amongst the flocks of developers.

It really highlighted how different open source projects can be when you start contributing to them. Looking back, it was clear why we spent a good chunk of time on the “Project Evaluation” part of the course and researching different projects before picking one.

The presentations were also a fun way to preview the variety of open source projects out there. In class, the presentations ranged from an education platform to a Twitch comment modifier - a clear demonstration that almost anything exists in the world of open source.
